ABOUT US

OUR STORY FAITH MISSION AND VISION TOGETHER

Calvary True Vine Gospel Church of Kenya was started in 2014 under the leadership of Bishop Kaloi and Rev. Rana Since its establishment, the church has continued to grow in faith and ministry, expanding from its original congregation into a wider network of 20 church branches.

The church is committed to spreading the Word of God, strengthening believers through worship and fellowship, and reaching out to communities through mission work, support for the needy and vulnerable, and community outreach activities. Over the years, the ministry has expanded to different locations, including branches such as Oloolabwak, Olomayiana, Narasha, Mpeuti, Nkampani, Sankale, Olaya, Ilkarampuni, Maela, Sanctuary, Ololoinyo D.C.K, New Jerusalem, Enchipai, Oloserian, Sonkoro, Olosho Rongai, Sambulai, Kimelok, Webuye, and Oldoinyio lole Kararo.

Today, with 20 branches, the church continues to grow and serve communities by providing a place for worship, spiritual growth, fellowship, prayer, and community support.

OUR MISSION

To spread the Gospel, strengthen believers, reach out to those in need, and positively transform communities through faith and service.

OUR VISION

To build a strong and growing church ministry that reaches people in different communities and brings them together in worship, fellowship, and service to God.
